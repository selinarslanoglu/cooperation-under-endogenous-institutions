Instructions

In this part of the experiment, you will participate with the same group in ten rounds as in Part 1 with the difference that the payoffs may be modified in the voting stage. If the payoffs are modified, the new payoffs will be as follows: If the person you are matched with chooses A, and you choose A, both of you earn 50 points. If the person you are matched with chooses B, and you choose A, you earn 10 points, whereas the other person earns 48 points. If the person you are matched with chooses A, and you choose B, you earn 48 points, whereas the other person earns 10 points. If the person you are matched with chooses B, and you choose B, you both earn 40 points.

Whether the payoffs are modified is determined as follows. First, every participant in your group will privately declare her preference to modify payoffs or not. Before that, you will be shown the past actions of each participant in your group. Second, the computer will randomly choose whether to consider the modification preferences or not in your group. If the computer considers the modification preferences then the majority wins, and in case of a tie, the computer will randomly decide to consider the preferences or not. If the computer does not consider the preferences in your group, it will randomly choose whether to modify payoffs or not.

When the voting stage is completed, you will participate in another ten rounds. In each round, you will be randomly matched with another participant in your group. If the outcome of the voting stage indicates modification of the payoffs, the payoffs will be determined according to Table 2. However, if the payoffs are not modified, the payoffs will be determined according to Table 1.
