Instructions

In part 1, you will be asked to make decisions in several rounds. For this part of this experiment, you will be randomly placed into a group of four people. Within this group, you will be randomly paired with another participant in each round. Everybody in your group has received the exact same instructions. However, it may be that people that are not in your group will participate in a different experiment.

There will be ten rounds in the first part of this experiment. In each round, you and the person you are matched with will simultaneously and independently choose one of two actions, A or B. The payoff you earn depends on both your action and the action of the person you are matched with.

If the person you are matched with chooses A, and you choose A, both of you earn 50 points. If the person you are matched with chooses B, and you choose A, you earn 10 points. whereas the other person earns 60 points. If the person you are matched with chooses A, and you choose B, you earn 60 points, whereas the other person earns 10 points. If the person you are matched with chooses B, and you choose B, you both earn 40 points. Table 1 given below summarizes the payoffs you and the other person you are matched with can get.
